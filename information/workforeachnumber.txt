NUMBERS AND YOU 
LLOYD STRAYHORN
1980

Choosing a Career

NUMBER 1 PERSON
Born on the 1st, 10th, 19th, or 28th, or if you’re a Leo or Aries,
you’ll find success in work as an administrator, athlete,
businessperson, director, doctor, explorer, hospital manager or
administrator, industrial engineer, instructor, inventor, laser
technician, lawyer, legal secretary, management trainer, microwave
technician, military officer, optometrist, politician, president,
salesperson, scientist, solar engineer, supervisor, union leader,
warden, or writer. Your best days for job interviews are Tuesdays
and Mondays. Over and above job experiences, sell your interviewer
on the fact that you can take the initiative, make decisions, and get
things done on your own.

NUMBER 2 PERSON
If you were born on the 2nd, 11th, 20th, or 29th, or if you happen
to be a Cancer, you’d do well to pursue a career as an actor,
architect, artist, bank teller, carpenter, caterer, chef, chemical
engineer, collector, computer analyst, computer programmer, cook,
dancer, diplomat, editor, electronic engineer, homemaker, mediator,
musician, nutritionist, poet, psychiatrist, psychologist, sailor,
secretary, statistician, systems engineer, teacher, or water operator.
Your best days for job interviews or for asking for a raise are
Monday and Friday. Beyond your job experience, let it be known
that you’re teamwork oriented, patient enough to learn the ropes,
and that you like cooperating with others for a common cause.

NUMBER 3 PERSON
Born on the 3rd, 12th, 21st, 30th, or under the signs of Pisces or
Sagittarius, success generally comes your way as an actor, animator,
artist, administrator, child care worker, court reporter, designer,
entertainer, fashion designer, gardener, government official, horse
trainer, illustrator, jeweler, judge, language interpreter, lawyer,
meeting or social planner, minister, photographer, publisher,
religious official, retail manager, socialite, speech therapist, writer,
veterinarian, or youth counselor. Your best interview days are
Thursday and Friday. In selling yourself, let the interviewer know
that you’re conscientious and hardworking, that you don’t mind
putting in long hours, and that you’re creative and optimistic when
it comes to solving problems and getting things done.

NUMBER 4 PERSON
If you were born on the 4th, 13th, 22nd, 31st, or if you’re an
Aquarius, you’ll find unusual success in careers as an accountant, air
traffic controller, auditor, aviator, broadcast technician, computer
expert, data processor, electrician, equipment repairer, forklift
operator, inventor, investigator, machinist, manufacturer, market
analyst, mathematician, mechanic, mechanical engineer, motorman,
musician, occultist, plumber, printer, radiologist, radio personality,
reformer, robotics technician, sculptor, or TV personality. Your best
interview days are Wednesday and Monday. In an interview,
emphasize your love of detail, that you like to be punctual (if you
sincerely mean it), and that you’re excellent at coming up with
different or unique ways of doing things.

NUMBER 5 PERSON
Born on the 5th, 14th, or 23rd, or if you’re a Gemini or Virgo, you
find personal satisfaction as an air-conditioning mechanic, aircraft
mechanic, astrologer, auto mechanic, aviator, buyer, copy writer,
crisis expert, detective, editor, gambler, importer/exporter,
investment analyst, medical technician, musician, news reporter,
private investigator, public official, promoter, racing driver,
respiratory therapist, salesperson, scientist, securities broker,
speculator, tour guide, travel agent, wholesaler, or writer.
Wednesday and Friday are your best interview days. Stress the fact
that you’re a fast learner and generally quick on your feet, as well as
the fact that you’re flexible and can easily adjust to new situations.

NUMBER 6 PERSON
If you were born on the 6th, 15th, or 24th, or you happen to be a
Taurus or Libra, look for fields of endeavor as an advisor, artist,
athlete, baker, barber, beautician, buyer, cashier, civil servant,
counselor, dental hygienist, designer, dentist, doctor, educator,
employment interviewer, fashion designer, health physicist, home
health aide, homemaker, host or hostess, hotel manager, lawyer,
musician, nurse, occupational therapist, physical therapist,
professor, property manager, realtor, school counselor, singer, social
worker, surgeon, teacher, teacher’s aide, or waiter. Your best
interview days are Friday and Thursday. Let your interviewer know
you’re dependable and responsible and not afraid to take on the
burdens that go with those traits. You have a sense of justice and
fair play, and know the difference between right and wrong.

NUMBER 7 PERSON
If you were born on the 7th, 16th, or 25th, or if you’re a Pisces or
Cancer, you’re likely to find career enjoyment as an accountant,
actor, analyst, artist (the spiritual kind), as an expert on a
specialized subject, astrologer, computer programmer, dream
analyst, forester, healer, historian, intelligence analyst, lab
technician, land surveyor, librarian, map maker, minister, navigator,
occultist, photographer, poet, psychiatrist, researcher, sailor,
scientist, secret agent, shorthand reporter, surgical technician,
technician, undersea explorer, or writer. Your best interview days
are Monday and Friday. During your interview, emphasize the fact
that you’re discriminating and discreet; that you can do a task or
assignment without being told or prodded, that you can easily work
alone if you have to.

NUMBER 8 PERSON
Those born on the 8th, 17th, or 26th, or under Capricorn, can be
a success as an auditor, authority, banker, boxer, brick mason,
builder, cemetery worker, church official, composer, controller,
corporate lawyer, criminal expert, farmer, financial expert,
government official, insurance underwriter, mathematician, miner,
minister, organizer, paralegal, parole officer, philosopher, police
officer, property manager, public official, real estate appraiser,
realtor, repairman, or tax preparer. Your best interview days are
Thursdays and Fridays. Aside from your past work experience,
you’re serious-minded, loyal, determined, and respected by others.
Remember, in your case, others need a little warm up time before
they really appreciate you.

NUMBER 9 PERSON
If you were born on the 9th, 18th, or 27th, or if you’re an Aries or
Scorpio, you can find personal success in jobs and careers as an
athlete, auto body repairer, ballistics expert, barber, computer
operator, construction worker, correctional officer, explosives
expert, fireman, glazer, gun collector, international personality,
iron-worker, lawyer, leader, locksmith, marksman, metallurgist,
metal worker, military officer, nuclear engineer, organizer,
politician, publisher, steelworker, surgeon, or tool and die maker.
Your best interview days are Tuesday and Thursday. Let it be known
that you have self-confidence (not egotism), and an ability to inspire
others and to get along with everyone you meet.
